# Sales Analytics – SQL & Power BI
This is my first data analytics project, where I worked with a sales dataset using PostgreSQL and SQL to explore the data and answer different business questions. I then used Power BI to visualize the results and create a sales dashboard.

## Tools Used
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Power BI
- pgAdmin

## Analysis
In this project, I explored:
- Sales performance by state
- Sales trends over time
- Top customers by revenue
- Product category performance
- Profit by category
- Sales by sub-category

## Power BI Dashboards

### 1. Executive Overview
Overview of revenue, profit, quantity sold, and profit margin over time.

![Executive Overview](executive-overview.png)

### 2. Product & Seasonal Insight
Shows profit margin by sub-category and compares August vs. December 2022 to highlight how higher sales do not always lead to higher profit.

### 3. Customer & Regional Insight

## What I Learned
This project helped me practice SQL aggregation, grouping and filtering, while also learning how to turn query results into clear visualizations in Power BI.
